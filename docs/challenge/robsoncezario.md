## Building Jungle Gaming

**Original games. One connected iGaming operation.**

[Jungle Gaming](https://junglegaming.io) is a B2B iGaming company combining original content, game aggregation, modular operating technology and AI-driven player intelligence.

We are building:

- Original slots and instant games with distinctive creative direction
- A catalogue of **30,000+ games from 120+ providers** through one integration
- A modular platform connecting player accounts, wallets, payments, CRM, gamification, affiliates and analytics
- Player-intelligence workflows that turn approved behavioral signals into relevant actions and measurable outcomes

Operators can adopt one capability or connect the ecosystem as their operation evolves.

## From code to company

My background is in full-stack engineering, software architecture and technology leadership across iGaming, crypto, financial platforms and e-commerce.

Today, my work sits where company strategy, product and engineering meet:

- Turning complex industry problems into focused product decisions
- Designing systems for reliability, observability and long-term ownership
- Building teams that understand the reasoning behind the architecture
- Using AI as engineering leverage through clear specifications and reviewable workflows
- Connecting technical decisions to player experience and business outcomes

I believe strong products come from clear context, explicit trade-offs and systems that teams can understand—not just operate.
